

# Drawing a Triangle

Similar to the last task, you now have to draw a Triangle. The triangle will have a base of 7 characters and the top of the triangle will be centred and only consist of 1 character. It will consist of `*` characters or `_` whitespace.

The height of the triangle will be 4 characters high. Each row of the triangle will consist of 2 less `*` which will be replaced by whitespace.
